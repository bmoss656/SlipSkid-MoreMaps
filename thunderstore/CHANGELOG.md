# Changelog

## 0.1.0
First release: four tracks.
- **Biome Blender** (Aqueduc): sand, mud, dirt, asphalt, ice and magma in one lap.
- **Choose Wisely** (Umbrellas): two forks, each a quick risky road against a slow safe one.
- **Kill Me** (Taiga): ice and reversed steering (reverse needs Good Cards; ice without it).
- **The Cannon** (Core 7 meltdown): a giant cannon that fires you down the track.
